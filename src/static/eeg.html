FREQUENCY SUMMARY

Average power spectrum

Each 4-second window has its mean removed. Power is averaged across valid windows; band shares are relative to total 1–40 Hz power.

—

Low-frequency power can be dominated by drift or movement; higher frequencies can include nearby muscle activity. Review the waveform before comparing activities.

AWEAR's public specifications give one behind-ear channel at 256 Hz. They do not define a voltage conversion, electrode reference, onboard filtering, or whether each record timestamp marks its first sample. Times inside a record are displayed assuming the timestamp is its start. These charts are for exploratory comparison; muscle activity, movement, and contact changes can affect them.
