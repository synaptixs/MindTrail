SIGNAL QUALITY

Windows needing review

A window can have more than one flag. Flags use thresholds relative to this archive; they are screening cues, not verified artifact diagnoses. Gaps and device changes are never joined into analysis windows.

PATTERN SHAPES

Median spectral shares

High low-frequency power may reflect drift or movement; higher frequencies may include muscle activity. Pattern numbers are unnamed signal groups.

MODEL COMPARISON

GMM and K-means

The timeline uses a two-group Gaussian mixture model as a coarse starting view. Silhouette measures feature separation; lower BIC favors a GMM fit. Neither score establishes an activity or brain state.
